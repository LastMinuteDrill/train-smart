Add to your home screen

Train Smart then opens like an app, straight from its icon.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

The home screen app keeps its own sign-in, so enter the password once more the first time you open it.

It also keeps its own record, separate from Safari. If you've used Train Smart in Safari, back up first (My record, Record tools, Download backup), then open the app and use Restore from backup.

Android

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install or Add.
